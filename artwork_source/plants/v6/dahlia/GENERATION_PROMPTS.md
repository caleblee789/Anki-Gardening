## Runtime processing and QA notes

- Seed uses connected chroma extraction at thresholds 80/80, pure-magenta
  soft-matte despill, and a geometry-only vertical scale of 0.75. Premultiplied
  alpha resizing preserves its warm-brown shell and exact soil-contact row.
- Sprout, Young, and Mature use border auto-key, soft matte, and despill.
- Flowering and Rare use connected chroma extraction at thresholds 45/45 and
  pure-magenta soft-matte despill so burgundy and plum petals are preserved.
- The Rare structure is deliberately different from Flowering: one dominant
  double crown plus three unequal satellite blooms on an irregular exposed
  branch fan, with readable negative-space channels. Palette and motes are
  secondary identifiers, not the structural distinction.
- Final standardized review sheets for Dahlia and the other nine approved lines
  are in `build/twilight-full-library-review/`.
