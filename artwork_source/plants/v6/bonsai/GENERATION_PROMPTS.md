# Bonsai V6 ImageGen prompts

Mode: built-in ImageGen. Mature was generated as a new image; the other stages
were generated as reference-based edits to preserve line continuity. Every image
used a flat magenta chroma-key background before local alpha extraction.

## Mature

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: mature-stage direct-soil plant sprite for the Anki Garden game
Primary request: create a mature bonsai line plant, growing directly from soil with no container. It should be an established miniature tree with one centered visible woody root flare and a graceful gently curved trunk, balanced asymmetrical branching, and several clearly separated rounded foliage pads. This is the Mature stage: healthy and established, but restrained enough that Flowering can become the natural full-form peak.
Style/medium: polished hand-painted storybook gouache matching a cozy twilight garden game; softly textured brushwork, clean readable silhouette, natural botanical detail, slightly whimsical but not cartoon-flat.
Composition/framing: single plant centered on a square canvas, upright front three-quarter view, centered narrow soil-contact base at the bottom, generous transparent-key padding on every side.
Color palette: deep sage, olive, and muted forest-green leaves; warm brown bark with subtle twilight-cool shadowing.
Scene/backdrop: one perfectly uniform solid pure magenta #FF00FF chroma-key background for removal. Every gap between trunk, branches, roots, and foliage must show that same flat magenta.
Constraints: plant only; no pot, planter, dirt mound, soil patch, rock, floor, cast shadow, text, logo, or watermark. The magenta background must have no gradient, texture, lighting variation, halo, haze, or reflection. Do not use magenta in the plant. Crisp edges and generous padding.
```
