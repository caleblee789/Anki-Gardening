# Lavender redesign, 5 September 2026

All six stages have been redrawn as one silver-green and violet storybook botanical line. The original Seed floated above the soil, the middle stages were sparse at normal garden size, and Full Bloom was narrower than Flowering. The replacement gives every stage a different silhouette, seats the seed and stems at the soil contact, and reserves the broad, densely flowered crown and three small glints for Full Bloom.

The runtime stage key `rare` still means **Full Bloom** in the interface. Canonical asset IDs and filenames are unchanged. The six runtime WebPs are installed in the working tree; the shared manifest and installer remain owned by the coordinating task and must receive the six replacements in `integration-handoff.json` together.

These are visible-art measurements, not the square texture size. All six masters and lossless RGBA WebPs are 1254 × 1254. Full Bloom is 106.1% of Flowering's width, 107.1% of its height, and 113.6% of its bounding area, within the existing 108% / 108% / 115% limits. Aspect-normalized confident-alpha silhouette IoU is 0.6498, within the provenance range of 0.12–0.82.

The seed retains its natural low silhouette rather than being stretched to a proposed 24 px height. Ground anchors use the opaque underside or stem foot; the thumbnail crop and optical center use the whole visible artwork. The scene's scale correction and the existing inverse-square-root thumbnail calibration are preserved. No independent thumbnail-scale override is introduced. Runtime contact shadows remain renderer-owned, using the supplied contact/base bounds and zero shadow offset; directional scenery lighting stays in the shared renderer.

**Integration:** `integration-handoff.json` contains six asset-ID-keyed replacement entries, each with the hash of the prior manifest entry. Hash encoding is sorted-key compact JSON in UTF-8. It also supplies the versioned source overrides, species scale corrections, and complete placement overrides for the coordinator's installer integration. Apply only these six entries after checking the prior hashes. Preserve the supplied thumbnail bounds, optical centers, safe padding, stem anchors, and support bounds when regenerating the catalog. The old unversioned Lavender chroma masters are retained for historical compatibility; the new alpha PNGs are the authoritative sources for this version.

**Source provenance:** `generation-prompts.json` retains all creative prompts. Mature v3 established the selected line; Young, Sprout, Seed, Flowering, and Full Bloom were generated using the line references. Mature v1 and v2 are discarded drafts, retained for provenance. `drafts.json` records the selected original outputs. Native transparency was inconsistent, so the user-authorized existing cleanup scripts removed only backgrounds and corrected alpha. `prepare_alpha.py` and `alpha-processing.json` record the exact processing. There was no scripted repainting, reshaping, or recoloring of the plants. `export_runtime_and_handoff.py` creates the lossless runtime assets and calibrated metadata.
