# Anki Garden 2.2.3

Hovering a planted bed now outlines both the plant and its planter in pale mint. Selecting a plant leaves only its bed outlined in green, while another plant can still show a temporary hover preview. Switching plants, entering the plant card, and entering a locked bed clear stale hover outlines.

The review session summary now opens as a compact 296 × 40 panel and expands on demand. The expanded receipt presents its rewards and plant progress directly, without the extra Growth breakdown control. Its placement also updates when the card itself changes size.

Saved Growth, rewards, balances, plant positions, and progression rules are unchanged. The declared compatibility range is desktop Anki 26.09.2 and newer. Exact-package native Anki verification remains pending.
