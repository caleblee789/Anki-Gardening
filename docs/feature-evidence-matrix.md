# Schema 14 and Verdant Twilight V6 evidence matrix

This is the current release contract for Anki Garden 2.1. Automated evidence is
necessary but not sufficient: live acceptance must use the exact rebuilt
archive in a fresh, uniquely named, sync-disabled Anki 26.08 base/profile.

| Feature | Intended behavior | Automated evidence | Live acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Startup and hooks | Start with or without an open collection; register menu, reviewer, sync, and home hooks once. | Startup, home, and storage tests. | Clean launch, one Tools action, no add-on warning. |
| Review ingestion | Each supported answer is counted once and gives 10 base Growth to the answer-time nurtured unfinished plant. A bounded scheduler-day ID ledger, rather than only a scalar maximum, reconciles late lower-ID sync rows exactly once. | Engine, reviewer, ledger migration/restart, normalization, and atomic rollback tests. | Answer Again and Good; compare Anki, home, Garden, plant card, and schema 14 JSON. |
| Same-day catch-up | Diff the authoritative `[scheduler-day start, cutoff)` revlog rows against the persisted ID ledger in revlog order. Prior-day and future/device-skew rows are neither counted nor cursor-consumed. | Bounded storage query, DST/cutoff, lower-ID sync, routing-period, and idempotence tests. | Seed or sync current-, prior-, future-skew, and out-of-order rows; refresh twice. |
| Nurture routing | Learner action is **Nurture**; internal `active_plant_id` and `active_plant_periods` route only future Growth. Growth is never split, moved, or overflowed after Rare. | Routing-period, switching, fully-grown, terminology, and collection tests. | Nurture different plants between answers and inspect both totals. |
| Growth stages | Seed, Sprout, Young, Mature, Flowering, and Rare use current thresholds; 25/50/75/100 feedback and stage Garden Coins are idempotent. | Threshold, stage-local display, transition, reward, and asset tests. | Cross progress and stage thresholds with motion on and off. |
| Anki streak | A missed Anki day resets the next streak to day 1; tiers add 0/5/10/15/20/25% Growth at days 1/7/14/30/100/365. | Exact boundary, reset, fractional-rounding, Garden-strip, and home tests. | Study across thresholds, miss a day, and compare the displayed bonus. |
| All due | Live collection-wide due set includes due reviews and introduced learning/relearning through cutoff, including filtered decks; unseen new and unavailable suspended/buried cards are excluded. At least one answer; once per day; no revocation. | Due-tree/SQL semantics, queue cases, restore-before-award, idempotence, and fail-closed tests. | Exercise review, learning, new, filtered, suspended, and buried cards. |
| Garden Coins | Internal `currency_balance` and ledger remain compatible; all learner-facing UI says Garden Coins. No per-answer Coins. All due, streak milestones, and stages record reason, delta, and resulting balance. | Terminology, ledger, duplicate-event, debit, reward-table, and simulation tests. | Earn each reward type, inspect Progress and Nursery, then restart. |
| Fresh starter | Schema 14 starts with no plants, two unlocked direct-soil spaces, and `starter_selection_complete=false`; first Open Garden auto-opens Nursery and one release-ready starter is free. Preselection reviews never receive plant Growth or later backfill. | Fresh-state, starter, routing-sentinel, migration, home, and dashboard tests. | Answer before selection, open Garden, choose starter, and inspect both spaces and persisted state. |
| Nursery landmark | Manifest geometry resolves only for registered action `garden.nursery.open`; it is clickable and keyboard accessible only in the full Garden, hover/focus glows, registered tooltip says **Open Nursery**, and movement disables it. | Landmark registry, geometry, accessibility, dashboard dispatch, and preview-mode tests. | Activate by mouse, Enter, and Space; confirm no home-preview hotspot. |
| Data-driven stock | A species is stocked only when every stage has a local release-preferred Verdant Twilight V6 `direct_soil` asset with valid geometry metadata. The current bundle stocks Bonsai, Rose, Sunflower, Lavender, Hydrangea, Peony, Foxglove, Japanese Maple, Wisteria, and Dahlia. Existing owned plants remain usable even when not stocked. | Complete/partial/missing-line, manifest, legacy-owned, and package tests. | Compare Nursery with manifest-ready, incomplete, and seeded legacy-owned plants. |
| Nursery collection | **Your plants** and **Available now** use computed counts without a roster denominator. Purchases, shelving/replanting, and next-space unlocks are transactional. | Catalog-summary, price, capacity, placement, insufficient-funds, and rollback tests. | Buy/shelve/plant, unlock spaces three through six, and exercise rejected operations. |
| Three-metric Garden strip | Permanent strip contains only Plant Growth, Anki streak, and Garden Coins. | Dashboard source, terminology, layout, and render-state tests. | Inspect with a nurtured plant, no eligible plant, and narrow/wide windows. |
| Progress drawer | Progress begins collapsed and owns Today, Achievements, Collection, detailed Growth, all-due status, and recent Garden Coin activity. | Visibility, content ownership, no-duplicate-copy, and responsive tests. | Expand/collapse and verify permanent Garden content stays concise. |
| Plant selection | Artwork-bound hover is restrained; one selected card shows identity, Growth, answers remaining, Fertilizer status, and Nurture/Fertilize/Move/Story. | Interaction-state, hit geometry, payload, wording, and card-position tests. | Mouse and keyboard pass with one to six plants and overlaps. |
| Direct Move and Undo | Move highlights valid scene spaces; selecting one immediately saves an empty move or occupied swap. Escape/Cancel exits before placement; save failure rolls back; Undo restores the last commit. No destination dropdown or Done action. | Placement engine, scene, rollback, source-contract, and keyboard tests. | Mouse and keyboard move/swap/cancel/undo pass. |
| Fertilizer cards | Three cards show exact Garden Coin cost, direct Growth per answer, and duration. Bonus applies only when `started_at <= answer_time < expires_at`; same tier preserves and extends one interval, while replacement or expired repurchase retains the prior bounded interval so late synced answers use the answer-time tier. | Price, card-copy, pre/during/post synced-row, timer, expiry, extension, replacement, interval-history, restart, migration, cap, and rollback tests. | Activate, sync an older answer, extend, replace, force expiry, and restart. |
| Plant Story | Compact hero, inline rename, semantic memories oldest to newest, warm early-story state, and **Up next** persist without storing study content. | Rename, ordering, next-milestone, serializer, privacy, accessibility, and empty-state tests. | Rename, advance a stage, inspect timeline, and restart. |
| Settings | Verdant Twilight is a read-only current-style card; live preview is noninteractive; Garden display and Motion are primary; Fine tune is collapsed; Restore defaults stages; Save and Cancel are explicit; Troubleshooting remains. | Config validation, dirty-state, preview, cancel/default/save, responsive, accessibility, and rollback tests. | Stage/cancel/save/default settings at wide and compact sizes, then restart. |
| Home preview | Deck Browser and Overview show only scene art, nurtured-plant Growth, Anki streak, Garden Coins, and **Open Garden**. Plants and Nursery are noninteractive. | Home phases, bridge, metric ownership, accessibility, fallback, and responsive tests. | Exercise both home surfaces, enable/disable, open, refresh, and error state. |
| State compatibility | Schema 10 is backed up and converted; schemas 11–13 migrate to schema 14 as established gardens. Legacy Fertilizer begins no earlier than migration time, and the legacy scalar cursor is converted to a retryable, atomic scheduler-day ledger seed. Corrupt/unsupported data is backed up. | Migration matrix, two-restart ledger preservation, late lower-ID arrival, activation-floor, repair, backup, round-trip, and rollback tests. | Seed schemas 10–13 and invalid JSON; inspect backups, sync an out-of-order row, and restart twice. |
| Verdant Twilight V6 | `verdant_twilight_surface_v6` supplies six direct-soil support lines/contact planes, responsive 4:3/16:9/home masters, occlusion, and Nursery geometry. | Asset audit, release-readiness, geometry/layout, render, and deterministic-build tests. | Inspect all spaces, stages, aspect ratios, occlusion, and interaction in the exact archive. |

## Explicitly deferred

Additional themes, decoration purchases, deck mapping, focus timers, exam mode,
social/cloud features, plant death/regression, passive rewards, and separate
rare-variant progression are outside schema 14. Future scene landmarks may use
the generic action registry, but only `garden.nursery.open` ships now.
