# Schema 21 rewards, Garden Finds, commerce, and Verdant Twilight V6 evidence matrix

This is the current release contract for Anki Garden 2.1. Automated evidence is
necessary but not sufficient: live acceptance must use the exact rebuilt
archive in a fresh, uniquely named, sync-disabled Anki 26.08 base/profile.

| Feature | Intended behavior | Automated evidence | Live acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Startup and hooks | Start with or without an open collection; register menu, reviewer, sync, and home hooks once. | Startup, home, and storage tests. | Clean launch, one Tools action, no add-on warning. |
| Review ingestion | Each eligible answer is counted once. The answer-time nurtured unfinished plant receives the committed normal-answer Growth award; every other planted unfinished plant receives an exact 20% allocation carried in fifths. Stable answer identities and lineage aliases reconcile sync and reanswer events once. | Engine, reviewer, reward-ledger migration/restart, lineage, normalization, and atomic rollback tests. | Answer Again and Good; compare Anki, home, Garden, plant card, and the schema-21 reward database. |
| Same-day catch-up | Diff the authoritative `[scheduler-day start, cutoff)` revlog rows against the persisted ID ledger in revlog order. Prior-day and future/device-skew rows are neither counted nor cursor-consumed. | Bounded storage query, DST/cutoff, lower-ID sync, routing-period, and idempotence tests. | Seed or sync current-, prior-, future-skew, and out-of-order rows; refresh twice. |
| Nurture routing | Learner action is **Nurture**; internal `active_plant_id` and `active_plant_periods` route only future full awards. Existing Growth never moves. Normal answers preserve passive exact-fifths fan-out; Garden Find Growth is direct to the nurtured plant only. | Routing-period, passive-accounting, direct-Find, switching, fully-grown, terminology, and collection tests. | Nurture different plants between answers; inspect nurtured, passive, residual, and direct-Find totals. |
| Growth stages | Seed, Sprout, Young, Mature, Flowering, and Rare use current thresholds; 25/50/75/100 feedback and stage Garden Coins are idempotent. | Threshold, stage-local display, transition, reward, and asset tests. | Cross progress and stage thresholds with motion on and off. |
| Anki streak and recurring rewards | Authoritative history reconstructs the active streak and 0/5/10/15/20/25% Growth tiers at days 1/7/14/30/100/365. The first eligible answer each active day grants 2 Garden Coins; every seventh day grants 10, with day 7 integrated into its one-time achievement. No Permanent Streak XP or variable daily Coin track exists. | History reconciliation, tier boundaries, daily/seventh-day idempotency, day-7 integration, reset, fractional rounding, Garden strip, and home tests. | Seed history across thresholds, sync, and compare the bonus plus committed reward receipts. |
| All due and achievements | The live due set includes due reviews and introduced learning/relearning through cutoff, including filtered decks; unseen new and unavailable suspended/buried cards are excluded. At least one eligible answer; once per day; no revocation. The ordinary 10-Coin result stacks with the first live-only 5-Coin All Clear achievement. Every achievement requirement and reward comes from the shared registry. | Due-tree/SQL semantics, achievement registry/history/finalization, stacking, idempotence, and fail-closed tests. | Exercise due queue classes; inspect one stacked first All Clear receipt and the registry-driven achievement cards. |
| Garden Coins and receipts | `currency_balance`, normalized reward/transaction rows, and bounded grouped receipts remain authoritative; all learner-facing UI says Garden Coins. Daily/seventh-day rewards, All Clear, achievements, stages, environment effects, and Garden Finds record durable event and correlation identities. | Terminology, ledger integrity/import, stacking, duplicate-event, debit, presentation-projection, and restart tests. | Earn stacked reward classes, inspect Progress/reviewer/Nursery, restart, and confirm no duplicate grant. |
| Fresh starter | Schema 21 starts with no plants, neutral environment entitlements, two unlocked direct-soil spaces, and incomplete starter setup. The flow says the cost is free, the choice creates one plant instance, and future qualifying answers generate Growth. Earlier answers do not receive retroactive Growth or recurring rewards; reliably reconstructable one-time achievements remain eligible for the separate history reconciliation. | Fresh-state, starter, routing-sentinel, migration, home, dashboard, copy, rollback, and responsive tests. | Answer before selection, choose/place/nurture a starter, reconcile history, and inspect both spaces, Growth, recurring receipts, achievements, and persisted state. |
| Scene landmarks | Manifest geometry registers Nursery and cottage actions only in the full Garden. Separate forgiving hit bounds and silhouette polygons provide shaped hover/focus outlines and in-scene labels; movement disables them. | Landmark registry, geometry, accessibility, dashboard dispatch, and preview-mode tests. | Activate both with mouse, Enter, and Space; confirm no home-preview hotspots. |
| Data-driven stock | A species is stocked only when every stage has a local release-preferred Verdant Twilight V6 `direct_soil` asset with valid geometry metadata. The current bundle stocks Bonsai, Rose, Sunflower, Lavender, Hydrangea, Peony, Foxglove, Japanese Maple, Wisteria, and Dahlia. Existing owned plants remain usable even when not stocked. | Complete/partial/missing-line, manifest, legacy-owned, and package tests. | Compare Nursery with manifest-ready, incomplete, and seeded legacy-owned plants. |
| Nursery catalog | Non-shrinking scrollable tabs separate Plants, Fertilizers and boosts, Garden beds, and Weather and Scenery. Cards use normalized artwork/fallbacks, computed stock/counts, textual owned/locked/active states, shared effect descriptors, and canonical Buy/Use/Unlock/Place in Garden/Store plant labels. Small/Standard Charges are repeat purchases; environment purchases are one-time and never auto-equip. | Catalog-summary, tabs, shared descriptors, action vocabulary, environment/Charge price, preview crop/fallback, capacity, empty state, and responsive tests. | Browse art, purchase/move/plant a species, purchase/use Charges, purchase an environment, unlock beds, and exercise rejected operations. |
| Purchase transactions | Every Garden Coin purchase uses an engine quote and shared confirmation. Confirmation revalidates price, balance, availability, ownership, target, Fertilizer, and sequential-bed state; one atomic save covers debit, acquisition/application/unlock, feedback, and bounded replay record. Exact request replay is idempotent; conflicting ID reuse fails closed. | Purchase and Growth Charge migrations, every purchase kind, typed stale/error status, rollback, persistence retry, double-submit, replay/conflict, receipt, keyboard, scroll/footer, and responsive tests. | Confirm each purchase kind; force every typed failure path and uncertain retry; verify unchanged committed state, receipt, restart, and no duplicate debit. |
| Three-metric Garden strip | Plant Growth and Anki streak include relative mini bars; all three button-like cards open focused, read-only detail dialogs. | Dashboard source, dialog, terminology, layout, focus, and render-state tests. | Open each detail by mouse/keyboard with active, empty, and full-grown states. |
| Garden Progress | Header Garden Progress opens its last valid page, defaulting to Plant Growth; header Collection and the cottage reuse that window and select Collection. Its five pages are Plant Growth, Anki Streak, Garden Coins, Achievements, and Collection. Collection shows ownership, textual Equipped state, shared exact mechanics, locked silhouettes, odds, and Ultra pity, and owns reversible loadout previews plus atomic equipment and visibility changes. | Routing, duplicate-activation guard, shared catalog descriptors, Collection mutation/rollback, no-duplicate-copy, and responsive tests. | Open through all three routes; inspect every owned/locked/equipped mechanic; preview, apply, cancel, and unequip from Collection; close with Escape and verify focus recovery. |
| Plant selection | Artwork-bound hover is restrained; one selected card shows identity, Growth, answers remaining, Fertilizer/Booster status, and Nurture/Fertilize/Growth Charge/Move/Story. | Interaction-state, hit geometry, payload, wording, and card-position tests. | Mouse and keyboard pass with one to six plants and overlaps. |
| Direct Move and Undo | Move highlights valid scene spaces; selecting one immediately saves an empty move or occupied swap. Escape/Cancel exits before placement; save failure rolls back; Undo restores the last commit. No destination dropdown or Done action. | Placement engine, scene, rollback, source-contract, and keyboard tests. | Mouse and keyboard move/swap/cancel/undo pass. |
| Fertilizers and boosts | Basic, Quality, and Magical Fertilizer cards show exact cost/effect/duration. Non-purchasable Booster Potions add +5 Growth for two hours, stack/extend, and preserve answer-time history. Small/Standard Charges add 100/500 immediate Growth. Grand adds 2,000 if present in imported development state but is not currently obtainable. Every Charge caps at Rare and consumes atomically. | Price, item art/copy, interval, stacking, duration passives, Charge transitions/cap, imported-state use, and rollback tests. | Activate/extend timed items and use Small/Standard across a stage; confirm Grand has no acquisition path; force expiry/save failure and restart. |
| Environment effects | Exactly one Weather and one Scenery effect stack. Hiding art does not disable them. Existing daily scenery gifts require an eligible answer, keep a separate reward identity, and do not suppress either Garden Find pool. | Exact-limit, stacking, all-due, stage-coin rounding, Booster-duration, daily-gift, visibility, reward-stacking, and separate Growth-source tests. | Equip combinations, hide each layer, answer across gift/Find/all-due/stage boundaries, and inspect one grouped result. |
| Garden Finds | Each newly processed eligible answer independently checks a Standard pool and an unowned-environment pool. Standard chance rises from 1/100 to 1/40 to 1/20, guarantees the 75th drought answer, and caps at three hits per day; registry weights choose Coins, direct Growth, Small or Standard Growth Charges, Basic Fertilizer, or Booster. Environment tiers retain 1/5,000, 1/20,000, and stepped Ultra pity. | Registry validation/fallback, chance boundaries, guarantee, cap, eligibility, direct Growth, consumable grants, stacking, environment ownership/pity, stable identity, migration, and 100k simulation tests. | Seed each registry outcome in a disposable profile; verify grouped receipt, direct Growth semantics, Collection/inventory, pity, restart, sync, and no replay. |
| Plant Story | Enlarged actual-stage art, explicit name/species/stage, relative Growth bar, inline rename, semantic memories, and **Up next** persist without storing study content. | Rename, ordering, next-milestone, serializer, privacy, accessibility, and empty-state tests. | Rename, advance a stage, inspect timeline, and restart. |
| Settings | Weather/Scenery, art quality/detail/performance, and Fine tune controls are absent. Balanced art and OS reduced-motion behavior are automatic; **Reduce animations**, **Show reviewer rewards**, and other applicable display/notification choices keep explicit Save/Cancel. Production **Diagnostics** is read-only; backup/populate/restore tools are enabled only in an isolated capture build. | Config, source/UI contract, production/capture capability, preview, cancel/default/save, dev-backup, responsive, accessibility, and rollback tests. | Confirm mutation controls are absent from the exact production package; exercise populate/restore only in a disposable capture build, then restart. |
| Home preview | Deck Browser and Overview show a fixed-height compact named scene, nurtured plant, relative Growth, and **Open Garden**. Today, Anki streak, and Garden Coins are intentionally absent, and scene objects remain noninteractive. | Home phases, height/split, bridge, information model, CTA geometry, accessibility, fallback, and responsive tests. | Exercise both home surfaces at narrow and ultrawide sizes. |
| State compatibility | Schemas 10–20 migrate fail-closed through the established onboarding, purchase, loadout, and Growth-accounting boundaries. Schema 21 imports the exact JSON state into a verified SQLite reward database, normalizes unbounded idempotency authorities, preserves a pre-SQLite backup, and rejects unreadable authority without overwrite. | Migration matrix, JSON-to-SQLite import, restart, concurrent revision, ledger integrity/backup, malformed/duplicate records, repair, and rollback tests. | Seed established schema-20 and invalid database families; inspect backups, imported reward history, replay, sync, and restart. |
| Verdant Twilight V6 and reskins | The canonical V6 surface supplies six direct-soil support lines/contact planes, responsive masters, occlusion, and Nursery/cottage geometry. Eight Scenery reskins reuse those exact placements, alpha masks, and hotspots; seven balanced Weather overlays compose over every setting. | Asset audit, placement-ref, alpha-mask identity, scenery resolution, release-readiness, geometry/layout, and deterministic-build tests. | Inspect all nine settings, seven Weather layers, six spaces, stages, aspect ratios, and landmark interaction. |

## Explicitly deferred

Additional geometry-changing themes, decoration purchases, deck mapping, focus
timers, exam mode, social/cloud features, plant death/regression, login-only
rewards, purchasable Boosters, and separate rare-variant progression are outside
schema 21. Four-theme integration and its downstream palette matrices are also
outside this release; the established Garden styling remains unchanged.
