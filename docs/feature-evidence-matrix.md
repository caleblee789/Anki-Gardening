# Schema 16 Weather, Scenery, and Verdant Twilight V6 evidence matrix

This is the current release contract for Anki Garden 2.1. Automated evidence is
necessary but not sufficient: live acceptance must use the exact rebuilt
archive in a fresh, uniquely named, sync-disabled Anki 26.08 base/profile.

| Feature | Intended behavior | Automated evidence | Live acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Startup and hooks | Start with or without an open collection; register menu, reviewer, sync, and home hooks once. | Startup, home, and storage tests. | Clean launch, one Tools action, no add-on warning. |
| Review ingestion | Each supported answer is counted once and gives 10 base Growth to the answer-time nurtured unfinished plant. A bounded scheduler-day ID ledger, rather than only a scalar maximum, reconciles late lower-ID sync rows exactly once. | Engine, reviewer, ledger migration/restart, normalization, and atomic rollback tests. | Answer Again and Good; compare Anki, home, Garden, plant card, and schema 17 JSON. |
| Same-day catch-up | Diff the authoritative `[scheduler-day start, cutoff)` revlog rows against the persisted ID ledger in revlog order. Prior-day and future/device-skew rows are neither counted nor cursor-consumed. | Bounded storage query, DST/cutoff, lower-ID sync, routing-period, and idempotence tests. | Seed or sync current-, prior-, future-skew, and out-of-order rows; refresh twice. |
| Nurture routing | Learner action is **Nurture**; internal `active_plant_id` and `active_plant_periods` route only future Growth. Growth is never split, moved, or overflowed after Rare. | Routing-period, switching, fully-grown, terminology, and collection tests. | Nurture different plants between answers and inspect both totals. |
| Growth stages | Seed, Sprout, Young, Mature, Flowering, and Rare use current thresholds; 25/50/75/100 feedback and stage Garden Coins are idempotent. | Threshold, stage-local display, transition, reward, and asset tests. | Cross progress and stage thresholds with motion on and off. |
| Anki streak | Authoritative review history reconstructs the current consecutive Anki-day run; tiers add 0/5/10/15/20/25% Growth at days 1/7/14/30/100/365 and previously reached Coin milestones claim once. | Retrospective query/reconciliation, exact boundary, reset, fractional-rounding, Garden-strip, and home tests. | Seed review history across thresholds, sync, and compare the displayed bonus and one-time claims. |
| All due | Live collection-wide due set includes due reviews and introduced learning/relearning through cutoff, including filtered decks; unseen new and unavailable suspended/buried cards are excluded. At least one answer; once per day; no revocation. | Due-tree/SQL semantics, queue cases, restore-before-award, idempotence, and fail-closed tests. | Exercise review, learning, new, filtered, suspended, and buried cards. |
| Garden Coins | Internal `currency_balance` and ledger remain compatible; all learner-facing UI says Garden Coins. All due, streak milestones, stages, and deterministic rare 50-Coin gifts record reason, delta, and resulting balance. | Terminology, drop caps, ledger, duplicate-event, debit, reward-table, and simulation tests. | Earn each reward type, inspect Coin details and Nursery, then restart. |
| Fresh starter | Schema 16 starts with no plants, neutral environment entitlements, two unlocked direct-soil spaces, and incomplete naming/starter setup; first Open Garden names the Garden, then opens Nursery for one free starter. Preselection reviews never receive plant Growth or later backfill. | Fresh-state, naming, starter, routing-sentinel, migration, home, and dashboard tests. | Answer before selection, name Garden, choose starter, and inspect both spaces and persisted state. |
| Scene landmarks | Manifest geometry registers Nursery and cottage actions only in the full Garden. Separate forgiving hit bounds and silhouette polygons provide shaped hover/focus outlines and in-scene labels; movement disables them. | Landmark registry, geometry, accessibility, dashboard dispatch, and preview-mode tests. | Activate both with mouse, Enter, and Space; confirm no home-preview hotspots. |
| Data-driven stock | A species is stocked only when every stage has a local release-preferred Verdant Twilight V6 `direct_soil` asset with valid geometry metadata. The current bundle stocks Bonsai, Rose, Sunflower, Lavender, Hydrangea, Peony, Foxglove, Japanese Maple, Wisteria, and Dahlia. Existing owned plants remain usable even when not stocked. | Complete/partial/missing-line, manifest, legacy-owned, and package tests. | Compare Nursery with manifest-ready, incomplete, and seeded legacy-owned plants. |
| Nursery catalog | Tabs separate Plants, Supplements & Boosters, Permanent Upgrades, and Weather & Scenery. Plant pages use computed stock/counts and stage artwork. Small/Standard Charges are repeat purchases; environment purchases are one-time and never auto-equip. | Catalog-summary, tabs, environment/Charge price, preview crop, capacity, insufficient-funds, and rollback tests. | Browse art, buy/shelve/plant, buy/use Charges, buy an environment, unlock spaces, and exercise rejected operations. |
| Three-metric Garden strip | Plant Growth and Anki streak include relative mini bars; all three button-like cards open focused, read-only detail dialogs. | Dashboard source, dialog, terminology, layout, focus, and render-state tests. | Open each detail by mouse/keyboard with active, empty, and full-grown states. |
| Garden Progress | Header Progress opens Overview; header Collection and the cottage reuse that same window and select Collection. The window contains Today, Achievements, Collection, Weather & Scenery, and How it grows. The environment tab owns loadout, layer visibility, all effects/acquisition, locked silhouettes, exact odds, and Ultra pity. | Routing, duplicate-activation guard, catalog, loadout, visibility, content ownership, no-duplicate-copy, and responsive tests. | Open through all three routes; rapidly activate the cottage; equip one of each, toggle visual layers, inspect locked entries/odds, close with Escape, and verify focus recovery. |
| Plant selection | Artwork-bound hover is restrained; one selected card shows identity, Growth, answers remaining, Fertilizer/Booster status, and Nurture/Fertilize/Move/Story. | Interaction-state, hit geometry, payload, wording, and card-position tests. | Mouse and keyboard pass with one to six plants and overlaps. |
| Direct Move and Undo | Move highlights valid scene spaces; selecting one immediately saves an empty move or occupied swap. Escape/Cancel exits before placement; save failure rolls back; Undo restores the last commit. No destination dropdown or Done action. | Placement engine, scene, rollback, source-contract, and keyboard tests. | Mouse and keyboard move/swap/cancel/undo pass. |
| Supplements | Basic, Quality, and Magical Fertilizer cards show exact cost/effect/duration. Non-purchasable Booster Potions add +5 Growth for two hours, stack/extend, and preserve answer-time history. Small/Standard/Grand Charges add 100/500/2,000 immediate Growth, cap at Rare, and consume atomically. | Price, item art/copy, interval, stacking, duration passives, charge transitions/cap, migration, and rollback tests. | Activate/extend timed items, use each Charge across a stage, force expiry/save failure, and restart. |
| Environment passives | Exactly one Weather and one Scenery passive stack. Weather effects remain minor; powerful Scenery is rare/drop-only. Hiding art does not disable passives. Daily gifts require an answer and consume the reward slot. | Exact-limit, stacking, all-due, stage-coin rounding, Booster-duration, daily-gift, visibility, and separate Growth-source tests. | Equip combinations, hide each layer, answer across limits/all-due/stage boundaries, and inspect Growth details. |
| Reward engine | One deterministic ordered band may win per eligible answer: Ultra environment 1/100,000 with stepped no-guarantee pity; Grand 1/30,000; Very Rare 1/20,000; Standard 1/8,000; Rare 1/5,000; Booster 1/5,000; Small 1/2,000; 50 Coins 1/800. Completed tiers fall back to Charges. | Exact-band, priority, determinism, duplicate, uniform-unowned, fallback, pity-threshold/reset, and daily-slot tests. | Seed each outcome in a disposable profile; verify notice/badge, collection/inventory, pity, restart, and no replay. |
| Plant Story | Enlarged actual-stage art, explicit name/species/stage, relative Growth bar, inline rename, semantic memories, and **Up next** persist without storing study content. | Rename, ordering, next-milestone, serializer, privacy, accessibility, and empty-state tests. | Rename, advance a stage, inspect timeline, and restart. |
| Settings | Weather/Scenery, art quality/detail/performance, animation, and Fine tune controls are absent. Balanced art and reduced-motion behavior are automatic; applicable display/notification choices keep explicit Save/Cancel. Production Troubleshooting is read-only; backup/populate/restore tools are enabled only in an isolated capture build. | Config, source/UI contract, production/capture capability, preview, cancel/default/save, dev-backup, responsive, accessibility, and rollback tests. | Confirm mutation controls are absent from the exact production package; exercise populate/restore only in a disposable capture build, then restart. |
| Home preview | Deck Browser and Overview show a compact named scene, nurtured-plant relative Growth, relative Anki streak, Garden Coins, and **Open Garden**. Scene objects remain noninteractive. | Home phases, height/split, bridge, metric ownership, accessibility, fallback, and responsive tests. | Exercise both home surfaces at narrow and ultrawide sizes. |
| State compatibility | Schema 15 gains the schema-16 environment state, then schema 16 upgrades to schema 17 with authoritative resumable onboarding. Older supported schemas preserve the established migration boundary. Corrupt/unsupported data is backed up. | Migration/onboarding matrix, environment/Charge round-trip, retrospective streak, ledger preservation, repair, backup, and rollback tests. | Seed empty, planted-incomplete, established schema-16, and invalid JSON; inspect backup/state, resume, sync, and restart. |
| Verdant Twilight V6 and reskins | The canonical V6 surface supplies six direct-soil support lines/contact planes, responsive masters, occlusion, and Nursery/cottage geometry. Eight Scenery reskins reuse those exact placements, alpha masks, and hotspots; seven balanced Weather overlays compose over every setting. | Asset audit, placement-ref, alpha-mask identity, scenery resolution, release-readiness, geometry/layout, and deterministic-build tests. | Inspect all nine settings, seven Weather layers, six spaces, stages, aspect ratios, and landmark interaction. |

## Explicitly deferred

Additional geometry-changing themes, decoration purchases, deck mapping, focus
timers, exam mode, social/cloud features, plant death/regression, login-only
rewards, purchasable Boosters, and separate rare-variant progression are outside
schema 17.
