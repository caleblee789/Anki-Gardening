# Schema 22 progression, Reviewer HUD, commerce, and Verdant Twilight V6 evidence matrix

This is the current release contract for Anki Garden 2.1. Automated evidence is
necessary but not sufficient: live acceptance must use the exact rebuilt
archive in a fresh, uniquely named, sync-disabled Anki 26.08 base/profile.

| Feature | Intended behavior | Automated evidence | Live acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Startup and hooks | Start with or without an open collection; register menu, reviewer, sync, and home hooks once. | Startup, home, and storage tests. | Clean launch, one Tools action, no add-on warning. |
| Review ingestion | Each eligible completed card is counted once. The nurtured unfinished plant receives full Answer Growth; every other planted plant creates an exact 20% Shared Growth share. Full Bloom shares divide among planted plants still growing, including the active plant. Stable internal event identities reconcile sync and repeated events once. | Engine, reviewer, reward-ledger migration/restart, lineage, redistribution, normalization, and atomic rollback tests. | Complete Again and Good cards with growing and Full Bloom plants; compare Anki, HUD, Garden, plant card, and the schema-22 reward database. |

## Explicitly deferred

Additional geometry-changing themes, decoration purchases, deck mapping, focus
timers, exam mode, social/cloud features, plant death/regression, login-only
rewards, purchasable Boosters, and separate rare-variant progression are outside
schema 22. Four-theme integration and its downstream palette matrices are also
outside this release; the established Garden styling remains unchanged. A
future **While You Were Gone** screen will present all rewards from cards synced
from another device; it is separate from and intentionally excluded from the
local Session Summary in this release.
