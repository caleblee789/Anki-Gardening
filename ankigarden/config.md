# Anki Garden settings

- **Daily growth goal**: Growth to aim for each day.
- **Show garden on home screens**: Show the garden card in Deck Browser and Overview.
- **Animate garden**: Enable gentle garden motion. Turn this off for reduced motion.
- **Theme**: Choose the garden artwork background.
- **Artwork quality**: Prefer faster, standard, or highest-detail local artwork.

Use **Tools → Anki Garden → Garden settings** for the guided settings and preview. The preview is noninteractive and does not change your garden until you save.
